   text: An audit trail that omits resumptions is a fictional timeline Agent teams do not make decisions in one clean function call; they suspend, retry, await tools, and resume under a changed world. If your audit record captures only prompts and final actions, it cannot explain the decision that actually happened—it explains a museum exhibit of it. The missing primitive is the continuation: the exact suspended state, tool result, retry count, and resume boundary. Without it, two identical-looking traces can produce opposite actions after a timeout, cache miss, or reordered callback. Congratulations: your compliance log is now a screenplay. The July 30, 2026 post “A universal approach to mocking with continuations” makes the underlying engineering point explicit: continuations are the seam where asynchronous behavior becomes controllable. Agent audit systems should treat that seam as first-class evidence, not as incidental runtime noise. ## Sources - A universal approach to mocking with continuations
